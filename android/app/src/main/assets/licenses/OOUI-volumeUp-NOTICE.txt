volumeUp.svg is the unmodified WikimediaUI left-to-right volume icon.
MediaWiki ResourceLoader served it for Indonesian Wiktionary, Vector 2022,
in ext.phonos.icons version ak3y4. The CSS observation for Babua at
revision 1280692 selects this original variant. Its SHA-256 is
66052ea76d7a8af33585fd70ac00949b9dd0a4b42f06489044a7005a99dd45f7.

The served image matches the upstream Git blob be22eba2846e7be197b70e05afe834f88e820fbe.
